─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Objective: Improve HONESTY of confidence scores (calibration, not magnitude)

Method: Fit sigmoid per ticker: P_calib = sigmoid(a * logit(P_pred) + b)

Result: ✅ Applied to production

================================================================================
KEY LEARNINGS
================================================================================

1. Sentiment Features: Appeared useful but were NOISY
   - 12 lag features had near-zero importance
   - Removing them: +39.3% relative improvement
   - Lesson: Backward elimination more effective than forward selection

2. Ensemble Averaging: Doesn't work when base models diverge significantly
   - Per-seed variance too high
   - Led to noise instead of complementarity
   - Lesson: Ensemble works best with diverse-but-accurate models

3. Per-Type Calibration: Breaks cross-ticker consistency
   - Improving one ticker (AAPL +1.67%) worsens another (BABA -4.39%)
   - Global threshold respects system-wide trade-off
   - Lesson: Universal hyperparameters often better than per-type tuning

4. Probability Calibration: Critical overlooked metric
   - ECE improvements huge (+0.3864) whereas recall unchanged
   - Confidence scores now HONEST (match actual accuracy)
   - Lesson: ROC-AUC not enough; calibration deserves attention

5. Segmentation Insights: STABLE >> VOLATILE
   - 3.42% confidence gap reflects inherent difficulty
   - NOT a model failure, but market reality
   - Lesson: Accept market constraints; optimize what's controllable
